Ad privacy on the EQLWiki test site

This notice applies only to advertising tests on test.eqlwiki.com. For general wiki practices, read the public wiki privacy policy. The advertising practices described here supplement that policy for this test site.

Advertising data and cookies

When advertising is enabled, Google and the ad providers identified in the consent message may receive information such as your IP address, the page URL and browser information. They may use cookies or similar storage for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and, subject to your consent choices where required, personalization. Advertising cookies may use prior visits to this or other websites to personalize ads.

Read how Google uses information from partner sites and use Google's ad personalization controls. The consent message identifies the selected ad providers and their purposes.

Your ad and consent choices

The wiki's Ads checkbox controls whether this integration loads advertising. Logged-out visitors start with Ads on; their choice lasts for the browser session. Signed-in accounts start with Ads off unless they have saved an On preference. That account preference applies while signed in and is saved for future visits. Logged-out and account choices remain separate. This preference is separate from consent to cookies, data use or personalized ads.

Choose Ads off to stop future ad loads through this integration. If ad code is already running, the page reloads when your Off choice can be saved. Other already-open tabs may continue until they recheck the preference; reload or close them to end their loaded ad code. If the choice cannot be saved, close the tab to end its loaded ad code.

Where the Google consent message is available, use Consent, Manage options or Do not consent to make your data choices. Revisit them through Privacy and cookie settings on ad pages once that control is available. Declining consent does not by itself promise that no ads appear or that no data is processed.
