Validation rules
----------------
1. Only labels beginning with “to” are treated as external route evidence.
2. Targets must match the exact zone/alias table. Substring guessing is disabled.
3. Multi-target labels joined by “&” or “and” are split and validated separately.
4. Labels that resolve only to expansions, internal rooms, or unknown zones are excluded.
5. A label on only one map proves the pair exists, but does not automatically prove a one-way mechanic.
6. Arrowheads are reserved for explicitly validated one-way portals/exits/firepots.
